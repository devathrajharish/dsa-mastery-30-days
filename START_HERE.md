## ❓ Common Questions

**Q: Can I do 2 days in one day?**  
A: Not recommended. 60 minutes is tight for deep learning. Better to do daily consistently.

**Q: What if I get stuck on a problem?**  
A: Follow the 20-minute rule:
1. Try 10 min independently
2. Write brute force solution (5 min)
3. Identify pattern (5 min)
4. Look at hint, not full solution
5. Re-solve next day

**Q: Should I memorize solutions?**  
A: NO! Learn patterns, not solutions. Every problem tests same patterns in different ways.

**Q: How do I stay motivated?**  
A: Push daily to GitHub. Track progress. Share on social media. Celebrate milestones!

**Q: What after Day 30?**  
A: Re-solve problems 2-3x. Then do 2-3 medium problems daily for 4 more weeks.
